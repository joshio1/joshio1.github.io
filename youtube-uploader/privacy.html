Youtube Uploader privacy policy

Youtube Uploader is a private tool used only by its owner to manage their own YouTube channels.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, it uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos and thumbnails to the owner's channels, set their titles and descriptions, and change their privacy status.

What it stores

The OAuth token Google issues is stored only on the owner's own computer. The tool collects no data from viewers or any other users, and shares no data with third parties.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Youtube Uploader's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It also follows the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
